$$\frac{dP}{P}=(\beta_{0}e^{-\alpha t})dt$$
$$\int  \frac{dP}{P}=\int  \beta_{0}e^{-\alpha t}~ d{t}\to \ln|P|=-\frac{\beta _0}{\alpha}e^{-\alpha t}+C$$
$$P=Ae^{ -\frac{\beta _0}{\alpha}e^{-\alpha t}}\to P(0)=P_{0}$$
$$P_{0}=Ae^{-\frac{\beta _0}{\alpha}}\to A=\frac{P_{0}}{e^{- \frac{\beta _0}{\alpha}}}=P_{0}e^{\frac{\beta _0}{\alpha}}$$
$$P(t)=P_{0}e^{\frac{\beta _0}{\alpha}}e^{ -\frac{\beta _0}{\alpha}e^{-\alpha t}}=P_{0} \exp\left( \frac{\beta _0}{\alpha}(1-e^{-\alpha t}) \right)$$
$$\lim_{ t \to \infty }P_{0} \exp\left( \frac{\beta _0}{\alpha}(1-e^{-\alpha t}) \right)= \lim_{ t \to \infty }P_{0} \exp\left( \frac{\beta _0}{\alpha}(1-0) \right)=P_{0}\exp(\beta_{0}/\alpha)$$
