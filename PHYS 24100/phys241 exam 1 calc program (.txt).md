DISCRETE CHARGE DISTRIBUTIONS:
F = kQq/r^2 = QE
p = qL
t = p x E
U = - p * e
dF/dq = k/r^2 (Q-2q) = 0 [maximized repulsion force]
---
ELECTRIC FIELDS:
phi = q/e0 (through surface)
phi = q/6e (through cube)
E = sigma/e0 (just outside conductor surface)

line charge of finite length:
E = Ex + Ey
Ex = k(lambda)(1/r2 - 1/r1)

E = kq/r^2 [far from finite line charge]
E = 2k(lambda)/r [infinite line charge]
E = kQz/(z^2+a^2)^3/2 [axis of charged ring]
E = kQ/z^2 [axis of charged disk]
E = (sigma)/2(e0) [infinite plane]
E = a(rho)/2(e0) [uniformly charged slab (a is slab length/2)]

spherical shell:
E = 0 [inside]
E = kQ/r^2 [outside (Q = 4(pi)(sigma)r^2)]

uniformly charged solid sphere radius R:
E = kQ/R^2 [r>R]
E = kQr/R^3 [r<R]

E = 4kq/(r^2(pi)) [2 curved rods]
2ErL(pi) = q/e0 [gauss law, charged cylinder]
E = r(sigma)/e0(x) [infinitely long cyl. wire]
---
ELECTRIC POTENTIAL
U = kq1q2/r [system U]
U = qV
V = k(rho)/r^2 [from dipole]
V = 2k|x|(pi)(sigma) ((1+r^2/z^2)^(1/2) - 1) [axis of disk]
V  = -2k(pi)(sigma)|x| + kq(1/r - 1/a) [point charge and sheet of charge]
V = 2k(lambda)ln(R_ref/R) [infinite line charge]

spherical shell:
V = kq/r [r >= R]
V = kq/R [R <= R]

E = -grad(V)
U = 1/2 qV [conductor, charge Q at V]
---
GEOMETRY
sphere:
SA = 4(pi)r^2
V = 4/3 (pi)r^3

cylinder
SA = 2(pi)r^2 + 2(pi)rh
V = pi(r^2)h

cube
SA = 6a^2
V = s^3
