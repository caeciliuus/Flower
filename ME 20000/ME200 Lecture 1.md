- System: portion of space/matter that we want to study
	- Surroundings: everything else

|                          | Open | Closed | Isolated |
| ------------------------ | ---- | ------ | -------- |
| Energy crosses boundary? | Y    | Y      | N        |
| Matter crosses boundary  | Y    | N      | N        |
- **Property:** measurable characteristic of a system with no knowledge of history (temperature, velocity,..)
	- Imagine new system with original and clone
		- **Intensive properties**: equal to those of original system
		- **Extensive properties**: properties are double those of original
	- **Specific property** is an extensive property per unit mass
		- **Specific volume** $v$ is reciprocal of density $\rho:$ $v=\frac{V}{m}=\frac{1}{\rho}$
- **Pressure**: force per unit area, $1Pa=1N/m^2$
	- Assume pressure at any point is $\equiv$ in all directions
	- We use absolute pressure -> measured relative to a vacuum
	- **Atmospheric pressure**: $p_{atm}=101.3kPa$
	- **Gage pressure**, $|p-p_{atm}|$, for system above atmospheric pressure
- Temperature is closely related to a gradient in the direction of natural heat transfer. Also an avgd. KE of molecules, atoms, or particles -> how fast the molecules in a material are moving
	- Assume when things are in thermal equilibrium, temperature is equal
	- A degree is defined as $\frac{1}{T_{2}-T_{1}}$
		- $1\degree F$ is $\frac{1}{180}$ of the $\Delta T$ between water freeze and boil
		- Absolute zero scales have $T=0$ at absolute zero
- **State**: list of properties that fully characterize a system
	- System in **internal equilibrium** **(1)** does not change when isolated, **(2)** has spatially uniform properties
	- Equation of state relates particles to one another
		- In an **ideal gas**, particles don't interact with each other
		- System of ideal gas has equation of state $pv=RT$
			- $R:$ 8.31 kJ/mol
			- $M=\frac{m}{n}$ is molar mass
			- $v$ is specific volume
- **Process**: transition between two equilibrium states
	- **Cycle**: process that starts/begins in same state
